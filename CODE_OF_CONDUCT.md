# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported
to the project maintainers at **mattia.aiolfi@bettinelli.it**. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect
the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
